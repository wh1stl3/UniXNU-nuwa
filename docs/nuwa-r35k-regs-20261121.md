# r35k: saved x29/x30 at SpringBoard breakpoint

Host-only replacement for r35j after deep review CHANGES REQUIRED. User-stack
reads, target-map references and map switches have been removed. For exact
SpringBoard `p_comm` and `EXC_BREAKPOINT`, the same target saved state supplies
PC, x29 and x30; only valid 64-bit states supply x29/x30. The exception ring
holds x29, x30 and validity separately. Row 6 prints `pc=` and `x30=`,
`x30=UNKNOWN` for unavailable 64-bit state, and zero as `x30=0`. Other
exceptions keep the old format; EX1–EX4 rows remain unchanged.

**x30 is the exception-time register**, not the saved value at `[x29+8]`:
the two are not interchangeable, and neither is guaranteed to identify the
requested caller. It is displayed as saved, without PAC stripping. The
exception ring holds complete code/subcode; if the X1 line would exceed 80
columns it displays `cs16=` with only their low 16 bits. Ring try-lock drops
and X1/EX PID correlation limitations from r35g still apply.

**deep_reviewer must re-review** the simplified exception path, including
whether any remaining operations can block. No device commands; authentication
NOT REACHED. Host measurements use 1440 px and 18 px/cell, not physical
scanout verification. Independent verifier must rerun the final build/tests.

## Host results (self-checked)

Replay measured 58 columns for the sample and 77 in the conservative
`INT_MAX`/all-64-bit-fields-max case; EX1–EX4's generated source is unchanged.

`0xfffffff00779e400` and `_boot_progress_exception` at `0xfffffff0079a76b8`.
The final disassemblies have the sole recorder call at `0xfffffff00779e680`.
The handler and recorder have no direct calls to user-copy, map or backtrace
functions; the recorder has no direct branches to other functions. This
direct-call check is not a transitive proof about the pre-existing handler's
